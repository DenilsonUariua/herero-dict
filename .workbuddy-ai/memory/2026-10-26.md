- Imported: DB 1671 → **2187 words** (34 clans + 473 surnames + 9 terms, all tagged `hangara-2017`). Lexicon 2187 entries / 2809 terms / 150 examples.
- Parser fixes along the way: (a) skip TOC lines (heading followed by a page number) and chapters with no surnames/emblem; (b) **never use "/" inside generated definitions** — the lexicon parser reads "EN/HZ" and turned surname lists into fake example sentences (examples spiked 150→643); (c) `parseDefinitions` now rejects no-slash fragments containing ':' or >8 words (prose, not terms); (d) clan/surname rows take their only term from `pronunciation`.
- Appwrite bulk delete gotcha: `tables.deleteRows(db, table, queries)` takes **queries**, not row ids (passing ids → "Invalid query: Syntax error"). Used `Query.contains('tags','hangara-2017')` to clear and re-import cleanly.
